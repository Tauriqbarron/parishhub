Guide for the parish office, ministry leaders and members

ParishHub

ParishHub keeps the parish's records in one place: people, households, sacraments, Mass attendance and ministries. It has two parts, and which one you use depends on your role.

Part Address Who uses it What it's for
ParishHub parishhub.net Parish office and clergy Parish records, sacraments, attendance, setting up ministries. See Part A.
ParishHub Ministries parishhub.net/ministries Ministry leaders and members Events, member lists and rosters for each ministry. See Part B and Part C.

The screenshots use a made-up parish with demo names. Your screens will show your own people and ministries.

Part A: For the parish office

ParishHub at parishhub.net holds the parish records. It works best on a computer. Only people whose email is on the approved list can sign in.

Sign in and the dashboard

  1. Go to parishhub.net and click Sign in with Google.
  2. Choose your approved Google account. If you see "Access denied. Your email is not authorized to use this application.", see Admin access.
  3. The Dashboard opens. It shows totals for people and households, this year's baptisms, marriages and deaths, a quick Sunday attendance box and shortcuts to common tasks.
  4. Use the menu on the left to move around. Sign out is at the top right.

Admin access: who can use ParishHub

ParishHub admins are the people who can sign in at parishhub.net. There's one level of admin. Every admin can see and change every record: people, sacraments, households, attendance, ministries and settings.

What you need A Google account, and its email address added to the ParishHub approved list.
Who adds or removes admins Your ParishHub administrator, the person who looks after the server. There's no screen in ParishHub for this.
How long it takes A few minutes once the administrator has your email. ParishHub restarts briefly, so choose a quiet time.
Does it give Ministries access? No. Being an admin doesn't make you a leader or member of any ministry. To use ParishHub Ministries, add yourself to a ministry like anyone else.

Getting a Google account

  1. A Gmail address, or a work address the parish runs on Google Workspace, already is a Google account. Skip to step 3.
  2. For any other email (for example an Outlook or parish address), go to accounts.google.com/signup, choose Use my current email address instead, and follow the steps. Google sends a code to that email to confirm it.
  3. Send your ParishHub administrator the exact email address of the Google account. It must match letter for letter.
  4. When they confirm, go to parishhub.net and sign in with that account.

When someone leaves: ask your ParishHub administrator to remove their email the same day. Once it's removed, they can no longer see or change any records, even if they still have ParishHub open.

Don't share an account. Give each person their own Google account, so you can remove one person's access without locking everyone else out.

Find a person

  1. Click People in the menu.
  2. Type a name or email in the search box.
  3. Click Filters to narrow the list by gender, age, living or deceased, household, or sacraments received or missing. For example, to list everyone baptised but not yet confirmed.
  4. Click the eye icon on a row to open that person. Don't use the pencil icon beside it; it leads to a page that doesn't exist yet.

Add a person

  1. Click Add Person on the People page or the dashboard.
  2. Enter the First Name and Last Name. You must fill in both.
  3. Click Add more details to add their date of birth, gender, email, phone and address. Always add an email for anyone joining a ministry; it must be the Google address they'll sign in with.
  4. To put them in a family straight away, tick Add to existing household and choose the household and their role.
  5. Click Save & View, or Save & Add Another to enter the next person.

The household list on this form only shows the first 20 households. If the family you want isn't listed, save the person, then add them from the household's own page (see Households).

A person's record

A person's page has four panels: Personal Information, Household, Sacraments and Family.

  1. Click Edit at the top to change their details, then Save.
  2. Use Add on the Household, Sacraments or Family panel to add to it. The small icons on each entry edit or remove it.
  3. Click Record Death when a parishioner dies (see Births and deaths).

People can't be deleted from the screen. If someone was entered twice, ask your ParishHub administrator to remove the duplicate.

Record a sacrament

  1. Open the person and click Add on the Sacraments panel.
  2. Choose the Sacrament Type and enter the Date Received.
  3. Type the godparents, sponsor, minister or witnesses into the Notes box. See the warning below.
  4. Click Add Sacrament. ParishHub checks the order: for example, First Communion can't be dated before Baptism.

Only the type, date and notes are saved at the moment.

The Godfather, Godmother, Sponsor, Minister, Bishop, Confirmation Name and Witness boxes look like they work, but what you type there is lost when you save. Until this is fixed, put those names in Notes, for example "Godparents: Luke Patel and Grace Kim. Minister: Fr Michael Byrne."

Marriages: if you pick the spouse, ParishHub links the couple and creates a new household for them. A message appears with Undo if you didn't want the new household.

Link family members

  1. Open one of the people and click Add on the Family panel.
  2. Search for the other person and choose the relationship type: Parent, Child, Spouse or Sibling.
  3. Click Add Relationship, then check the Family panel. The other person should appear under the right heading.

The relationship type describes the person whose page you're on, not the person you picked. The help text on the form says the opposite.

For example, to link a child to their father, open the child's page, pick the father and choose Child. If someone ends up under the wrong heading, remove them with the × and add them again with the other choice.

Households

  1. Click Households in the menu, then New Household. (The Add Household button on the dashboard only opens this list.)
  2. Enter a Household Name, such as "Tupou family", and the address. Start typing the address to pick it from the list. Click Create Household.
  3. On the household page, click Add Member, search for the person, and choose their role: Head, Spouse, Child or Other.
  4. To change someone's role later, click their role badge and choose the new role. It saves straight away.

People without their own address show the household's address on their record, marked "from household".

Record Mass attendance

There are two ways. The quick box on the dashboard records one number. The full form records several Masses on one date.

  1. Click Attendance in the menu, then Record Attendance.
  2. Pick the Date.
  3. For each Mass, choose it from Mass Time and enter the Count. Click + Add Another Mass Time for the next one.
  4. Click Record Attendance.
  5. To correct a number, find it on the Attendance page and click Edit.

Always pick a Mass from the list. "Other (specify)" doesn't save. If a Mass is missing, add it under Mass times first. Use the full form rather than the dashboard's quick box: numbers from the quick box may not show under "By Mass Time" in Analytics.

Record a birth or a death

  1. Birth: on the dashboard, click Record Birth. Enter the baby's name and date of birth, and search for one or both parents. Click Record Birth.
  2. ParishHub creates the baby's person record, links them to the parents and adds them to the parents' household. Don't add the baby under People first, or you'll get two records.
  3. Death: open the person and click Record Death, or use Record Death on the dashboard. Enter the date, and the funeral and burial details if you have them. Click Record Death.
  4. The person is then marked Deceased. Their record stays, with the death details on it.

Analytics

  1. Click Analytics. The cards at the top show births and deaths this year, average weekly attendance and total registered members.
  2. Use the tabs for Births, Deaths and Attendance.
  3. On Attendance, choose a period (1M, 3M, 6M, YTD, 1Y, 3Y or Custom). Switch to By Mass Time to compare Masses, then click a period button to load it.

The Population tab stays empty for now. There's no way to enter population figures yet.

Set up a ministry and its leaders

This is step 1 in how a ministry gets set up. Once a ministry has a leader, the leader adds members in ParishHub Ministries.

  1. Click Ministries, then New Ministry. Enter the Name and a short description, leave Active ticked, and click Create Ministry.
  2. Open the ministry. In Leadership, search for the leader and click them. The first person you add becomes the Leader; anyone after that becomes a Co-Leader.
  3. Open the leader's People record and check their Email is exactly the Google address they'll sign in with. If it's missing or different, they'll see "You haven't been added to any ministry yet."
  4. Send the leader this guide and the address parishhub.net/ministries.

Changing leaders: the remove icon takes someone off straight away, with no confirmation. You can't choose Leader or Co-Leader here. To swap them, remove both and add them back in the order you want. The leader can also change roles in ParishHub Ministries.

Closing a ministry: click Edit, untick Active and save. Delete warns that it removes everything, but a ministry with members or events is only made inactive.

Mass times

  1. Click Settings, then Manage Mass Times.
  2. Click Add Mass Time. Enter a name people will recognise, such as "Sunday 9am", the time and the day, then click Create.
  3. If a Mass stops, click Deactivate rather than deleting it, so past attendance keeps its name.

The registration form and QR code

New parishioners can register themselves at parishhub.net/register, as an individual or as a household, without signing in.

  1. Click Settings. Under QR Code Registration, check the Base URL is https://parishhub.net and click Save.
  2. Click Copy to copy the registration link for a newsletter or email, or Download QR Code to print it for the church noticeboard.
  3. Registrations go straight into People and Households, with no approval step. Check new entries each week for duplicates and missing details.

Some registration details aren't saved yet: the household's phone, email and state, and the sacrament details (church, minister, godparents, sponsors, witnesses). Sacrament types and dates are kept. Follow up with new families for anything you need.

ParishHub Ministries: start here

Parts B and C cover ParishHub Ministries at parishhub.net/ministries, the app for ministry leaders and members. The parish office uses it too when they belong to a ministry.

Leaders, co-leaders and members

Leaders and members all use ParishHub Ministries. What you can do there depends on your role in each ministry, and you can have a different role in each one. For example, you might lead the Readers and be a member of the Youth Group.

Sign in to ParishHub Ministries

  1. Go to parishhub.net/ministries. On a phone, you can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.
  2. Tap Sign in with Google.
  3. Choose the Google account with the email address the parish has for you. If you use a different email, the app won't recognise you.
  4. You'll land on My Ministries.

"You haven't been added to any ministry yet." This means no ministry has your email address yet. Ask your ministry leader to add you, or ask the parish office to check which email they have for you.

Finding your way around

There are four tabs along the bottom of the screen:

Ministries The ministries you belong to. Tap one to open it.
Rosters Your own roster slots, plus any swap requests sent to you.
Calendar Your events and roster slots, month by month.
Profile Your name, email and roles. You sign out here.

The bell at the top right shows your notifications. The number on it is how many you have.

Part B: For members

Everything in this part works the same for leaders and co-leaders.

See your ministry

  1. On the Ministries tab, tap a ministry.
  2. Events lists what the ministry has coming up. Tap an event to see its time, place and details.
  3. Members shows who is in the ministry, with their email and phone number.
  4. Roster then View Roster shows the upcoming rosters and any open slots.

Accept or decline a roster slot

When a leader puts you on a roster, the slot shows as Pending until you answer. Please answer quickly so your leader knows whether to find someone else.

  1. Tap the Rosters tab. Your slots are grouped by date.
  2. Check the Mass or event, the date and your part (for example First Reading · Reader).
  3. Tap Accept if you can do it. The slot changes to Accepted.
  4. Tap Decline if you can't. The slot goes back to the open list for someone else to take, and it disappears from your Rosters tab.

You can also accept or decline from the Calendar. Tap the roster entry on the day. The calendar calls a pending slot Tentative and an accepted one Confirmed.

Take an open slot

Open slots are listed under Available on your ministry's roster. When you take one, you're confirmed straight away and don't need to accept it.

  1. Open your ministry, tap Roster, then View Roster.
  2. Tap a roster to open it, if it isn't open already.
  3. Find a slot under Available that you're trained for. The grey word under each slot, such as Reader or Psalmist, is the role it needs.
  4. Tap Take Slot. Your name moves up to Assigned as Accepted.

"You do not have the required roster role for this slot"

Every slot needs a matching role, such as Reader. The Take Slot button shows on every slot, even ones you don't have the role for. If you see this message, tap Try Again to go back, then pick a different slot. Ask your leader if you think you should have that role.

Swap with someone

If you've accepted a slot and can no longer do it, you can ask someone to swap. Right now you can only swap with someone who already has another slot on the same roster. If nobody is listed, use Cancel instead and let your leader know.

  1. On the Rosters tab, find the slot and tap Swap.
  2. Choose the person you want to swap with.
  3. Add a short note so they know which day it's for, for example "I'm away on the 27th, could you take First Reading?" The request they receive doesn't show the date, so the note matters.
  4. Tap Propose Swap. Your slot stays yours until they accept.

If someone asks to swap with you, a Pending Swap card appears at the top of your Rosters tab.

  1. Tap the card to see who's asking and read their note.
  2. Tap Accept to take over their slot, or Decline. There's no "are you sure?" step, so check the note first.

If you can't make it

These buttons appear on a slot you've accepted:

Swap Ask a specific person to take it over. See Swap with someone.
Cancel Give the slot up. It goes back to the open list and leaves your Rosters tab straight away. Tell your leader, because nobody is covering it yet.
Mark Complete Only tap this after you've done it. The button works before the day too, so be careful.

None of these buttons asks "are you sure?".

Use the calendar

  1. Tap the Calendar tab. Today is circled. Use the arrows to change month.
  2. Roster slots are green once confirmed, and amber with a dashed edge while tentative.
  3. Tap an entry to see the details. For a tentative roster slot you can also accept or decline here.

Notifications and your profile

  1. Tap the bell to see notifications, such as a new roster opening or being added to a ministry. Use the buttons along the top to filter them.
  2. Notifications stay bold even after you've read them. That's a known fault and nothing is wrong on your side.
  3. Tap Profile to see which email you're signed in with. Tap Sign out if you share the device.

Part C: For leaders and co-leaders

You can also do everything in Part B. If you're a co-leader, read Notes for co-leaders first.

Your ministry page

Open your ministry from the Ministries tab. The Leader badge under the name confirms you have leader tools. Each tab has one extra thing for you:

  • Events has Add Event.
  • Members has Add Member, a role picker and roster roles for each person.
  • Roster has Create / Manage Roster.

Add an event

  1. On the Events tab, tap Add Event.
  2. Enter a Title and Date. You must fill in both.
  3. Add the start and end time, location and a description if you have them.
  4. Pick a Type. Leave Capacity empty unless numbers are limited.
  5. For a regular meeting, set Recurrence to Weekly, Biweekly or Monthly.
  6. Tap Create. The event appears on the Events tab and in members' calendars.

Don't use the red bin icon to delete events yet. It shows "Not Found" and the event stays. If you added an event by mistake, cancel it instead (see next section).

Change or cancel an event

  1. Tap the event on the Events tab to open its edit page.
  2. Change what you need, then tap Save Changes. A message confirms "Event updated".
  3. To call an event off, tick Cancelled and save. It then shows as cancelled on everyone's calendar.

Things to watch on this page

Check Type before saving. This page only offers Meeting, Social, Service and Other, so an Education or Sacrament event shows as Meeting. Emptying a box, such as the location, doesn't remove what was there before. Changing a repeating event changes every date in the series.

Add a member

You add people who are already in the parish records. If someone isn't found, ask the parish office to add them to ParishHub first, with their email address.

  1. On the Members tab, tap Add Member.
  2. Type at least two letters of their first or last name. Searching by email doesn't work, even though the box suggests it does.
  3. Tap the right person in the list. Check the email under their name.
  4. Leave Role and Joined Date as they are. New people always join as a Member, dated today. You can change their role afterwards.
  5. Tap Add. They can now sign in and see the ministry.

A person without an email in the parish records can't sign in. They'll appear in your member list, but the app has no way to recognise them. Ask the parish office to add their email.

Don't use the red bin to remove members yet. It can fail or remove the wrong person. Ask the parish office to remove someone for now.

Change a member's role

  1. On the Members tab, find the person.
  2. Use the drop-down under their name to choose Member, Co-Leader or Leader.
  3. The change saves as soon as you pick. There's no confirmation message, so check the badge at the top right of their card.

Anyone you promote should sign out and back in before the new leader tools appear on the roster screen.

Give someone a roster role

Roster roles, such as Reader, Psalmist or Prayers of the Faithful, say what someone is trained to do. A person can only be put on, or take, a slot that matches one of their roles.

  1. On the Members tab, find the person's Roster Roles row.
  2. Tap + Assign and tap the role to add it.
  3. To take a role away, tap the × on its blue chip. This happens straight away.

Roster roles are shared across the parish. Removing someone's Reader role here removes it from every ministry they're in. Check with the other leaders first.

Set up a roster template

A template describes a regular roster once, such as "Sunday 9am Mass: two readers, a psalmist and the prayers". You then make each week's roster from it. You only do this once per Mass or regular event.

  1. Open your ministry, tap Roster, then Create / Manage Roster.
  2. Tap + Template.
  3. Give it a Name people will recognise, like Sunday 9am Mass, and choose how often it happens under Rule.
  4. Tap + Add Slot once for each job. For each slot, pick the role it needs and type a label, such as First Reading. The two number boxes are the fewest and most people for that slot. Leave both as 1 for a single person.
  5. If the role you need isn't listed, tap + New Role to create it.
  6. Under Settings, leave Allow members to self-assign ticked if members may take open slots themselves.
  7. Tap Create Template.

This screen is easier on a computer or tablet, or with your phone turned sideways. On a phone the slot rows are wider than the screen, so the number boxes and the bin icon sit off to the right. You can reach them by scrolling sideways.

Slots show on the roster in alphabetical order, not the order you add them. Templates can't be edited or deleted in the app yet, so check the slots before you tap Create.

Create a roster

  1. On the roster screen, tap Create Roster.
  2. Choose the Template. The count in brackets always says "(0 slots)"; ignore it, because your slots are saved.
  3. Pick the Date. It starts on next Sunday.
  4. Keep "Publish immediately" switched on. A roster saved as a draft can't be published later in the app.
  5. Tap Create & Publish. The roster appears with every slot under Available.

Leave the Notes box empty. What you type there isn't saved. Put any instructions in an event description or tell people directly.

Put people on a roster

You can put people on a roster yourself, or leave slots open for members to take. People you put on show as Pending until they accept.

  1. On the roster, find the slot under Available and tap Assign.
  2. Type part of the person's name and tap them in the list.
  3. They move to Assigned as Pending, and it appears on their Rosters tab to accept.
  4. Tap the refresh arrows at the top later to check who has accepted.

"Person doesn't have the required roster role"

The person needs the role for that slot first. See Give someone a roster role. The error fills the whole screen; tap Try Again to get back.

In the app you can't take someone off a roster once they're on it. Ask them to tap Cancel on their slot, or contact the parish office.

Notes for co-leaders

Co-leaders can add events, add members and give roster roles, just like a leader. They can't make someone a leader; the Leader option is hidden.

Some screens still show co-leaders the member view:

  • Roster screen: no + Template, Create Roster or Assign buttons. Ask your leader to set up rosters and assign people.
  • Event page: shows the details but no edit form. Ask your leader to change or cancel an event.

This will be fixed so co-leaders get the full leader tools.

Problems and questions

I can't sign in, or it says I haven't been added

Make sure you chose the Google account with the email the parish has for you. If it still doesn't work, ask your leader whether you're on the member list, and ask the parish office to check your email address.

The sign-in page keeps coming back

Close the tab and open parishhub.net/ministries again. If that doesn't help, sign out from Profile, then sign in again.

I was made a leader but I don't see the leader buttons

Sign out from Profile and sign in again. The app picks up your new role when you sign in.

The person I want to swap with isn't in the list

You can only swap with people who already have a slot on the same roster. Either ask your leader to put them on, or tap Cancel on your slot and tell your leader.

Someone isn't found when I search to add them

Search by first or last name, not email. If they still don't appear, they aren't in the parish records yet. Ask the parish office to add them.

Where do I RSVP or record attendance?

These aren't available yet. Keep using your current way of counting who comes, for example a sign-in sheet.

Can I choose email or text notifications?

Not yet. The notifications you see come through the bell in the app.

I can't sign in to ParishHub (parishhub.net)

Only approved Google accounts can sign in there. See Admin access. Leaders and members use parishhub.net/ministries instead.

A leader says they can't see their ministry

Open their People record and check the email matches the Google account they sign in with. Then check they're listed under the ministry's Leadership.

Who do I contact?

For anything about your ministry, your leader. For accounts, email addresses or removing someone, the parish office.

Technical administrator

Appendix: adding or removing a ParishHub admin

For the person who runs the ParishHub server. Parish staff don't need this page.

Change the approved email list

The approved list is the AUTHORIZED_EMAILS setting in the server's .env file. The website and the backend both read it, and both check it on every request.

  1. Connect to the ParishHub server over SSH.
  2. Edit the canonical settings file /home/deploy/parishhub/.env. The deploy job copies this file, so edit this one, not the copy in the runner folder.
  3. Change the line AUTHORIZED_EMAILS=. Separate addresses with commas, for example AUTHORIZED_EMAILS=office@example.org,priest@example.org. To remove someone, delete their address. Keep at least one address, or nobody can sign in.
  4. Apply it by re-running the latest successful CD workflow in the repository's GitHub Actions. It copies the file and redeploys.
  5. Or apply it by hand, as the deploy user, in /home/deploy/actions-runner/_work/parishhub/parishhub: run cp /home/deploy/parishhub/.env .env, then docker compose -f docker-compose.prod.yml up -d --no-deps --force-recreate backend frontend.
  6. Check it worked: sign in with the added account and open People. For a removal, check that account now gets "Access denied".

Don't run docker compose down, and don't start ParishHub from any other folder. Compose would treat it as the same project and could stop the database. Recreate only backend and frontend, as above.
